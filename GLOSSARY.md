# Pennel

Pennel keeps meeting owners' written context separate from the transcript-based material they choose to share.

## Language

**Typed notes**:
The meeting owner's own written notes, shown as **Your notes**, distinct from recognized speech and a summary. They are private context.

**Personal summary**:
The meeting owner's working document, shown as **Summary**, which may combine typed notes and finalized transcript when generated. It remains separate from a shareable summary.
_Avoid_: Owner summary

**Shareable summary**:
A summary made only from permitted finalized transcript sources with known provenance. It excludes typed notes, personal summaries and material derived from private notes.
_Avoid_: Public summary, anonymized summary

**Incomplete transcript**:
A meeting transcript with recorded passages still awaiting successful transcription. The meeting's saved work and its transcription progress are separate states.

**Retry failed transcription**:
An explicit recovery attempt for previously unsuccessful passages using their retained audio, preserving completed transcript passages and the owner's typed notes.

**Retranscribe recording**:
An explicit new transcription pass over a retained recording, producing a revised transcript.

**Suggestion**:
A source-linked item proposed for the meeting owner's review that flags an action, decision, open question or changed commitment. Its quoted passage can contain several events, with unsupported owners or dates left unknown.

**Writing profile**:
A person's language-specific writing tendencies inferred from their own speech and corrected by them. It describes expression, rather than the sound of their voice or the facts they discussed.
_Avoid_: Speaking profile, voiceprint

**Speech sample**:
A passage of the owner's recognized speech admitted as evidence for a writing profile. A capture session can contain several samples without becoming several independent examples.

**Learned trait**:
A writing tendency supported by speech from several capture sessions, such as directness or sentence rhythm.

**Trait override**:
A person's explicit correction to a writing-profile trait, retained as further speech is learned.
