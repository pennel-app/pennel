# Pennel

Pennel keeps meeting owners' written context separate from the transcript-based material they choose to share.

## Language

**Typed notes**:
The meeting owner's own written notes, shown as **Your notes**, distinct from recognized speech and a summary. They are private context.

**Personal summary**:
The meeting owner's working document, shown as **Summary**, which may combine typed notes and finalized transcript when generated. It remains separate from a shareable summary.
_Avoid_: Owner summary

**Shareable summary**:
A summary made only from permitted finalized transcript sources with known provenance. It excludes typed notes, personal summaries and material derived from private notes.
_Avoid_: Public summary, anonymized summary
